# Srajan Agarwal
I’m a Data Science student at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, exploring how graph learning and geometric deep learning can help us decode the brain.
My research centers on scalable graph representation learning and computational neuroscience—focusing on compact graph representations, graph condensation, and cortical network modeling from source-localised neural dynamics.
