Modifications: vertices were spatially clustered, the left hemisphere was
mirrored to make an illustrative pair, and local surface edges were retained.
This is a conceptual rendering: colours, community groupings, interhemispheric
arcs, and signal pulses are illustrative and do not represent measured
connectivity or experimental data. It is not a validated anatomical viewer.

Other research graphics and the page design are original code-native visuals.
